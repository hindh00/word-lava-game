# Word Lava

Climb above the rising lava by chaining words together. Each word you enter must begin with the last letter of the previous word. Keep the chain going before the lava reaches you.

## How to Play

Start with the letter shown on screen, then enter an unused word from the game’s dictionary that begins with that letter. The last letter of your word becomes the next required letter. The counter below it shows how many unused words are available for that starting letter.

Your climb begins with the lava 10 metres below. Words raise your height and push the lava farther away, but the lava keeps rising and gradually speeds up.

## Scoring and Survival

- Each letter adds 1 metre to your height.
- J, Q, X, and Z add 3 bonus metres each. K and V add 1 bonus metre each.
- An invalid, repeated, or wrong-starting word surges the lava by 1 metre and resets your streak.
- Skip to a different available letter, but the lava surges by 3 metres.
- Use up to 3 hints per climb to reveal a possible word.
- Enter 5 valid words in a row to crust the lava over for 3 seconds.

The climb ends when the lava catches you. Your results show your height, number of words, longest word, and best streak. Your best height is saved in your browser.
